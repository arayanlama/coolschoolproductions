POLICIES

01—04
01

ARTIST FIRST

Artists retain ownership of their original work unless a separate written agreement says otherwise. Cool School does not claim rights simply because work is submitted, featured or reviewed.

02

TRANSPARENT SUPPORT

Any funding, production help, sponsorship or revenue arrangement will be disclosed clearly to the artist before work begins. No hidden deductions, ownership claims or implied obligations.

03

FAIR SUBMISSIONS

Submitting work does not guarantee selection, funding, release or representation. We review work on creative fit, feasibility and available resources, without charging a submission fee.

04

PRIVACY + CREDIT

Personal information shared with Cool School is used only for communication and project evaluation. Featured work will be credited to the relevant artists and collaborators wherever reasonably possible.
