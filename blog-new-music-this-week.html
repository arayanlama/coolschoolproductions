New Music
This Week.

Three songs on our radar this week — each memorable for a different reason.

COOL SCHOOL PRODUCTIONS
01PRATEEK KUHAD

You Make It Easy

A good song with lyrics that can resonate with almost everyone. Small details — like the number seven appearing in the song — give the writing more meaning, while the repetition makes its central feeling clear without overcomplicating it. It is simple, warm and easy to return to.

02FORREST NOLAN · FEAT. HEY HEY

my girl could too

This one leans further into an indie sound, but with a distinctly teenage energy. There are traces of punk and pop in it, giving the track a youthful, slightly restless character. Overall, it works — especially within Forrest Nolan’s Indie Movie Soundtrack world, a series they have also built a presence around on Instagram.

OPEN IN APPLE MUSIC ↗
03GRACE CARTER

Rather You Leave

The intro is beautiful — one of the best we have heard in a while. It catches you almost immediately and sets up the rest of the song with real confidence. This feels like one of those tracks that stays in your head long after it ends.
